# Messages inventory: bank sync, bills, statement reminder (D14)

Rule: every warning or error a person can see says **what** didn't happen, **why**, and **what they can do**. Raw text lives only in "Technical details" (Problems page, bank error page). All wording is built in `app/services/bankapi/messages.py` (reminder wording: `app/services/statement_reminder.py`). "Log" = recorded in the Problems page under the area shown, ref in brackets, cleared by itself when the same thing next works.

(link: bank page) | Bank sync `bank-connection:<id>`; cleared by a successful sync or by renewal |
