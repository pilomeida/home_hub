Home Hub: three UI options

Wireframe mock-ups, 12 desktop screens and 3 phone screens per option, sample data. Same decisions underneath (see ../ux-workflow-and-screens.md): Ask first, one Waiting queue, person-first Health, things as pages, people as a filter, settings behind a gear. No gradients, no faces.

Option A: Warm and soft

Friendly and roomy: large rounded cards, big numbers, hatched bars. The top bar is a row of pills. Best on a phone and for the children; the desk work is a little less dense.

Option B: Calm workspace

A left sidebar and white cards with one green accent. The familiar workspace layout; grows easily as categories are added; balanced between phone and desk.

Option C: Dense and precise

Top tabs, small type, tables and charts first, with hover values and bulk selection. The best operator desk; the phone version trades density for a short list.

Also: chart rules, how the scale adapts (the same chart on nine different datasets).
