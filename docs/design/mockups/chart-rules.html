Chart rules: one rule for every chart

One rule for every chart, whatever its type and whoever asks for it (a page of the Hub or a question to Ask): columns, history line, trendline, horizontal bars. Nothing is set per chart. The axis shows its real values. Its floor is worked out from the numbers: it shows the difference (40% of the spread below the smallest value) but is capped so that a visual change is never more than 1.5 times the real change (floor at most a third of the smallest value), then lowered to a tick. Numbers close together therefore sit on a near-zero axis and stay honest; numbers far apart can start higher. If the floor reaches zero, the axis starts at zero. The current month shows only what has happened so far (no forecast, no budget); past months show their full month and the same-day value. A reference range is a pale band behind the data (only the part inside the axis) and a value outside it is red. All figures are sample data.
