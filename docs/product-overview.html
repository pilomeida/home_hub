Home & Family Hub

The family's private filing cabinet that reads its own mail. Every bill, bank line, lab result and warranty lands in one place, gets recognised, filed and checked — and the family can simply ask it questions.

How it works

From a paper to an answer

Every paper takes the same road, whichever door it came through. A person always has the last word on anything the Hub isn't sure of.

The four areas

What it looks after

Each area knows its own kinds of paper: how to read them, what to file them as, and how private they are.

Money & Bills

  • Santander Totta, card and Revolut sync at 07:30, 13:00, 18:30 (Lisbon)
  • Every line filed on a 3-level spending tree (Food › Groceries › Supermarket)
  • Budgets to watch against; Coming up: what's due in the next 3 months
  • Loans & savings, with red-flag alerts; utility use charted (electricity, water, telecom)
  • Payments matched by Multibanco entity number, not by name
  • Daily cash check: the balance must match the bank, or it says so

Health

  • Upload asks only for the file — the Hub works out who and what
  • Records by person, by body area and by topic; appointments
  • Read twice and checked, benchmarked for getting it right first time
  • Always the highest privacy level, by design

Privacy

Three levels, set per kind of paper

Every AI call goes through one gateway, and every call must carry a level. If it doesn't, it is refused — the Hub fails closed. A nightly self-test proves the locks still hold.

🔓

House Low

Everyday papers. Cheapest suitable model.
🔐

House Mid

Only providers that keep no data, in the US or EU.
🔒

House High

Privatemode only (encrypted). No route available = blocked, never downgraded. Health lives here.

📭 No level yet?

Nothing may read it. You set it on the Privacy page.
