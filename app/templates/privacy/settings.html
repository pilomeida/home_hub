Documents the Hub recognises on its own

When a document matches one of these rules, the Hub knows what it is without asking an AI, and pre-fills the Inbox card. A rule only counts if every rule that matches a document agrees; otherwise you decide. Text comes from the subject, the caption or a PDF's own text. Photos and scans have no text of their own, so only the sender or your caption can recognise them. Hide personal details works only on text: documents sent to AI as PDFs or photos can't be hidden, and are flagged instead.
